# Employee Handbook 2026 (Halden Labs, fictional company)

Annual leave: every full-time employee gets 25 days of paid vacation per year, plus public holidays. Unused days up to 5 can be carried over into the next year. Vacation requests are made in the HR portal and are approved by your manager within 3 working days.

Parental leave: new parents receive 16 weeks of paid parental leave, and they can take it in one block or split it over the first 12 months. Parental leave is available to every parent, whatever their role or how the child joins the family.

Remote work: employees may work from home up to 3 days a week. Teams agree one anchor day each week for in-person meetings. Working from another country is allowed for up to 20 days a year and needs approval from your manager and from HR.

Expenses: submit expense claims within 30 days with a receipt. Meals while travelling are reimbursed up to 60 EUR per day. Flights and hotels are booked through the company travel desk, and anything over 500 EUR needs approval from your manager first.

Learning budget: every employee has a learning budget of 1,200 EUR per year for courses, books and conferences. Ask your manager before you book a conference.
