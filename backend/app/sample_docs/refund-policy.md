# Refund Policy (Halden Labs, fictional company)

Eligibility: customers can request a full refund within 30 days of purchase. After 30 days, refunds are made only when the product failed to work as described and our support team could not fix the problem.

Process: refunds are requested through the support portal with the order number. Support replies within 2 business days. Approved refunds are paid back to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days after approval.

Annual plans: if an annual plan is cancelled after 30 days, the customer keeps access until the end of the paid year and no further payment is taken. Partial-year refunds are not given unless the product was unavailable for more than 48 hours in a row.

Exceptions: custom work and onboarding services are not refundable once the work has started. Gift cards and promotional credits cannot be refunded as cash.

Escalation: if a customer disagrees with a refund decision, support escalates the case to the billing lead, who gives a final answer within 5 business days.
