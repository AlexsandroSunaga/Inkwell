# ACME Corp — Remote Work

Hybrid employees may work remotely up to **3 days per week** with manager approval.

- Core collaboration hours: **10:00–16:00** in your local timezone.
- Remote workers must have **25 Mbps** download and a dedicated workspace for video calls.
- International remote work longer than **14 days** requires HR and Legal review.
- Equipment stipend: **$500** one-time for home office setup (receipts required).

VPN must be connected when accessing internal dashboards or staging databases.
