# ACME Corp — Paid Time Off (PTO)

Employees accrue **1.25 days of PTO per month** (15 days per year) starting on their hire date.

- PTO requests must be submitted in the HR portal at least **5 business days** before the start date for trips longer than 2 days.
- Managers approve or deny within **48 hours**.
- Unused PTO above **5 days** may be carried to the next calendar year; maximum carryover is **10 days**.
- PTO does not accrue during unpaid leave.
